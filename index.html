Welcome, ruler!

Your island has a Town Hall and six villagers. Grow it into a mighty kingdom.

  1. Feed your people. Villagers eat food. Build a Farm or a Fishing Hut first.
  2. Gather materials. Put Lumber Mills next to forests and Quarries next to hills. More neighbors means more output.
  3. Build houses. Houses let more villagers move in, and buildings need villagers to work.
  4. Grow. More villagers unlock new buildings. Expand your borders and upgrade buildings to keep going forever.

Drag to turn the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two-finger drag to move. Your kingdom saves by itself.
