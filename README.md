# Tiny Kingdom

A 3D kingdom-building game that runs in your browser. Gather wood, stone, food and gold, grow your villagers, and keep expanding your island forever.

## How to play

- **Feed your people.** Villagers eat food, so build a Farm or a Fishing Hut first.
- **Gather materials.** Put Lumber Mills next to forests and Quarries next to hills. More neighbors means more output.
- **Build houses.** Houses let more villagers move in, and buildings need villagers to work.
- **Grow.** More villagers unlock new buildings at Town, City and Kingdom. Expand your borders and upgrade buildings to keep going.

Drag to turn the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag or two-finger drag to move. Number keys 1–9 pick buildings. Your kingdom saves in your browser automatically.

## Built with

- [three.js](https://threejs.org/) for the 3D world
- [simplex-noise](https://github.com/jwagner/simplex-noise.js) for island generation

Everything is in `index.html`. The game loads three.js and simplex-noise from cdn.jsdelivr.net.
